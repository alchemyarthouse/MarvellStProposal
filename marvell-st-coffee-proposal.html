The design process

How the logo revisions work

To keep things moving and give you real choice without dragging the timeline out, here's how we structure the design rounds.

1
Round one — 5 directions

We present a wide spread of logo directions in one round. You pick the one you're drawn to, and we talk through what you liked and didn't like across the others — that conversation shapes everything after it.

2
Rounds two & three — refine

We come back with the chosen direction refined. Three revision rounds in total are included, ending in final approval and a locked logo.

$
Beyond three revisions

Any revisions past the three included rounds are billed at an hourly rate and added to the final invoice.

⚠
On major changes of direction: a significant pivot mid-project — moving away from the agreed direction rather than refining it — will slow production down and could change the scope and cost of the project. We'll always flag this clearly before it affects the timeline or the bill.
